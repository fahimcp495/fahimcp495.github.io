About Me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate of the University of Dhaka and a competitive programmer. My interests are in algorithm engineering, combinatorial optimization, parallel algorithms and high-performance computing: how algorithms that are well understood in theory behave under real constraints such as limited time, memory and hardware.

I have solved more than 4,000 algorithmic problems, mostly in C++, which led to the Codeforces Master title (peak rating 2244) and several top finishes at the ICPC Dhaka Regional and inter-university contests. My final-year project on real-time stereo matching, and my time as a Machine Learning Engineer at Inverse AI (March 2025 to June 2026), showed me that practical performance depends on more than asymptotic complexity. At Inverse AI I implemented the FINALLY speech-enhancement model from scratch, released it as open source, and worked on training and inference efficiency.

I enjoy teaching. I have mentored more than 100 students in algorithms and problem-solving, and I have designed problems for national mathematics and AI olympiads. I am interested in pursuing doctoral research in algorithm engineering and high-performance computing.
